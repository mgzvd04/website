long story short, the CICC gave both Discord and Reddit a day to set up local offices, which, unironically is not the most realistic timeframe to take action. [this has now been extended](https://x.com/gmanews/status/2102761599748481181), but still, both Globe and DITO users are heavily affected by this as both telcos complied to this. Smart/PLDT and Converge users remain unaffected as of writing this.

# what do i do now?
for now, the only way to circumvent this is changing your DNS servers as most of people connected are using Globe/DITO's DNS servers from themselves, showing the warnings in their end.

# how do i do that?
this depends on platform you're currently using, so i'll leave there links on how to do that yourself:

- Windows 11: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m596Mz9gYMQ
- Windows 10 and below: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqTUk5GgmQ8
- macOS: https://support.apple.com/en-ph/guide/mac-help/mh14127/mac
- iOS: https://developers.cloudflare.com/1.1.1.1/setup/ios/
- Android: steps may differ a bit from brand to brand, but it's usually at Network & Internet/Connections > Private DNS. google search is also your friend, say like, "[brand + phone model] change DNS servers".
- Linux: idk, you probably gotta search it up. though I use Control D's daemon (see their Free DNS section and in the Setup Guide) if you don't want to go deep.

# which DNS server should i use?
note: all of these don't cost a penny!
## the usual ones
- Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1: https://developers.cloudflare.com/1.1.1.1/setup/
- Google Public DNS: https://developers.google.com/speed/public-dns/docs/using
## for privacy-oriented peeps
- Quad9: https://quad9.net/
- NextDNS: https://nextdns.io/
- Control D's Free DNS: https://controld.com/free-dns

# can't i just use a VPN instead? i'm too lazy for this.
yes. if you can't be bothered to do all of the above, Proton VPN is always there as the free tier is more than enough if you only use it for Discord. however there may be stuff like Steam Authenticator complaining why you're in the different location, so make sure to enable whenever needed. 

also see:
- [Windscribe](https://windscribe.com/): has a Build-Your-Plan starting $3, which is roughly 180PHP. you get to pick two locations of your choice plus unlimited bandwidth, and split tunneling is not locked behind a paid subscription. has 10GB in the free tier.

- [iVPN](https://www.ivpn.net/en/)/[Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en): on a much bit more expensive end, but offers all locations with just 5 euros per month (approx. 358 php). no need for the credentials like Calyx VPN as your account is the account number provided to you.
